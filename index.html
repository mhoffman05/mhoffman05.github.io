Projects

Running on Bad Data ↗

DCS 105 · Fall 2025

A website debunking sports science claims made to endurance athletes. It examines three products: Nomio, a broccoli-sprout supplement marketed with weak studies and an unlabeled graph; sodium bicarbonate from Maurten and Bicarb Shop, whose race calculators predict gains well beyond what controlled studies show; and Garmin and Coros watches, whose VO₂ max and race predictions are algorithm estimates presented as measurements. The site uses visual analysis, analysis in R, and public-facing explanation to refute each claim.

VECINA ↗

DCS 236 · Winter 2026

VECINA is a community resource platform run by the Woonasquatucket River Watershed Council for Providence, RI neighborhoods. On the class healthcare team, I helped draft a new healthcare section of the site: compiling and cleaning resource data in Google Sheets (addresses, contact information, English and Spanish descriptions, and map coordinates) and curating reliable sources for Vecinita, the project's AI assistant. The work was done with, not just for, the community it serves.

Experience

Bavis Lab, Bates College

Summer 2026

Summer research student, respiratory physiology

Studied breathing in neonatal rats across two projects. We found that the drop in ventilation after returning from hypoxia to normal oxygen was not matched by a drop in metabolic rate. I also worked on a rat model of SIDS combining inflammation (LPS) with sustained hypoxia. Methods included head-out plethysmography, injections, simple surgery, LabChart, and GraphPad Prism.
